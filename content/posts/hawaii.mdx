It was Ben's birthday, so we went to Hawaii for the first time. In this post, I'll walk you through what to see on the Big Island, plus a lot of cool stuff you should know about Hawaii.

The first mistake is taking only three days when you have fifteen activities planned. I booked the trip with an unspecified "Hawaii is so close, we can go anytime" feeling that my fellow Europeans flying to Paris just for a weekend can surely relate to.

Well, Hawaii is not close to San Francisco. It's just uncomfortably distant from anything, in the middle of blue emptiness. The Polynesians, the first people who arrived in Hawaii, had to build robust double-hulled canoes using coconuts and crab nails. They sailed to the islands intentionally and with new hope, escaping overpopulation and droughts in their homeland more than 2,000 miles away.
