The hypothesis, piece by piece

All nontrivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function have real part ½.

All Every one, including infinitely many unchecked cases.

Nontrivial Excluding the zeros −2, −4, −6, …

Zeros Inputs s whose output ζ(s) equals 0.

Riemann zeta function The function first defined by the infinite sum, then extended.

Have real part ½ Every zero has horizontal coordinate ½; their heights differ.
